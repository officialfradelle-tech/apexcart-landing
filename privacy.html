Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. Who we are

Paxora ("Paxora", "we", "us") is checkout software for Shopify stores, operated by Alvorix LTD, a company registered in England and Wales, United Kingdom. Paxora lets merchants accept payments through their own Stripe account and creates the paid orders in their Shopify store.

This policy explains what personal data we process, why, and the choices you have. Questions: support@paxora.io.

2. Our role

  • Merchants (businesses that sign up for Paxora): we are the controller of your account data.
  • Shoppers (people who buy from a merchant's store through the Paxora checkout): the merchant is the controller of your data. We process it on the merchant's behalf as a processor, only to run the checkout, create the order and provide the features the merchant turned on. For questions about an order, contact the store you bought from.

3. Data we process

Merchant accounts

  • Name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash) and sign-in method (email or Google).
  • Connections: your Stripe account ID and connection details, your Shopify store domain and API credentials (encrypted at rest), and checkout settings such as your logo, colours, language and reviews you add.
  • Order summaries shown in your dashboard (amount, currency, customer name and email, payment method).
  • Technical data needed to keep the service secure (IP address, browser, log records).

Shoppers

  • Contact and delivery details entered at checkout: name, email, phone number, shipping and billing address.
  • Cart contents, prices, shipping option and the resulting order.
  • Payment details are entered into secure fields provided by Stripe and go directly to Stripe. Paxora never sees or stores full card numbers or bank credentials.
  • If the merchant connected an advertising pixel (see section 5): IP address, browser user agent, advertising cookie identifiers (such as Meta's _fbp/_fbc) and ad click identifiers.

5. Facebook / Meta integration

Merchants can connect their Meta (Facebook) Business account with Connect with Facebook and choose a pixel (dataset). When they do:

  • We receive and store, encrypted, an access token issued by Meta for that merchant's business, plus the IDs and names of the pixels the merchant shared with Paxora.
  • We use this access only to send that merchant's own checkout events (InitiateCheckout, AddPaymentInfo, Purchase) to that merchant's pixel, through the Meta Pixel in the browser and the Meta Conversions API from our server. Shopper contact details sent to Meta (email, phone, name, city, postal code, country) are hashed with SHA-256 before they leave our servers.
  • We do not read the merchant's ads, audiences or other Meta data, and we never share data from one merchant with another.
  • The merchant can disconnect at any time in Paxora (Pixels → Disconnect) and remove Paxora in Meta Business Settings → Integrations. Disconnecting deletes the stored token and pixel selection.

The same principles apply to TikTok when that integration becomes available.

7. Cookies

  • Paxora dashboard (app.paxora.io): only essential cookies to keep you signed in and secure.
  • Paxora checkout: essential cookies from Stripe for secure payments. If the merchant connected a Meta pixel, the Meta Pixel may set advertising cookies on the checkout page. Merchants are responsible for obtaining any consent required for advertising cookies on their store.
  • This website (paxora.io): no advertising cookies.

8. How long we keep data

  • Merchant account data: while the account is active, and deleted within 30 days after you ask us to close it (except what we must keep for accounting, usually 6 years).
  • Order summaries and checkout records: as long as the merchant's account is active, or until the merchant asks us to delete them. Orders themselves live in the merchant's Shopify store and Stripe account.
  • Ad-matching signals (IP, user agent, cookie and click IDs) captured at checkout: up to 90 days.
  • Server logs: up to 30 days.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent. Merchants can do most of this from their dashboard or by emailing support@paxora.io. Shoppers should contact the store they bought from; we will help the merchant respond.

You can also complain to a data protection authority, in the UK the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), or the authority where you live.

10. Data deletion requests

To delete your data:

  • Merchants: email support@paxora.io from your account email with the subject "Delete my account". We delete your account, connections (Stripe, Shopify, Meta tokens) and stored data within 30 days and confirm by email. To remove only the Facebook connection, go to Pixels → Disconnect in Paxora.
  • Facebook users: if you connected Facebook to Paxora and want that data removed, email support@paxora.io or remove Paxora in Meta Business Settings → Integrations. We delete the related token and pixel information within 30 days.
  • Shoppers: contact the store you bought from, or email us with the store name and order email and we will forward your request.

11. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest for credentials (AES-256-GCM), strict access controls, and providers with strong security certifications. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in the app or by email. Contact: Alvorix LTD, United Kingdom · support@paxora.io.
